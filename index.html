River Valley Walk: Easy Views, Big Payoff

Best for: sunsets • Time: 60-90 minutes

Edmonton's river valley has long trails, viewpoints, and peaceful spots close to downtown. Start with a short loop and being a warm layer - weather changes quickly.

Outdoors Scenic Beginner-friendly

Whyte Avenue Evening: Food, Shops, and People-Watching

Best for: cafes • Time: 2-3 hours

Whyte Avenue is a classic for browsing local shops and finding great food. Pick one dessert place, one dinner spot, and walk the strip.

Food Local Night Out

Muttart Conservatory

Best for: exploration • Time: 2-3 hours

Come explore the pyramids with 700 different kinds of plant species and botanical gardens in four unique pyramids, surrounded by music, nature and beautiful works of art.

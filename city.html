Edmonton at a Glance

Edmonton is a vibrant city that combines natural scenery with urban attractions, making it a great destination for short trips or weekend travel. The river valley offers extensive trails and viewpoints that fell surprisingly close to downtown. The city also has lively neighbourhoods with ocal restaurants, cafes, and shops that are easy to explore on foot.

What Visitors Enjoy

  • Enjoy Nature in Parks
  • Vibrant Festival Scene
  • Hockey and Football Teams
  • Shopping

Best Time to Visit

Edmonton has all four seasons. Come in the summer for warm temps. Come in the fall and see the leaves change colors or come in the winter to see a white winter land.

One-Day Itinerary

  1. Start the morning exploring the River Valley
  2. Visit the Muttart Conservatory
  3. Spend the afternoon at the Royal Alberta Museum
  4. Finish the day with dinner and walk along Whyte Avenue
